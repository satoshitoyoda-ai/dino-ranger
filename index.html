Field Guide

🎙 Ranger lines in your voice

Tap ● Record, say the line (the example, or anything you like), then ■ Stop. Your recording plays instead of the built-in ranger voice at that moment in the game. Recordings stay on this device only. Tap ▶ to hear a line; 🗑 removes your recording and brings the built-in ranger back.

Grown-ups

Most natural voice on iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (or Spoken Content) → Voices → English → download a Premium or Enhanced voice (e.g. Ava, Zoe, Evan, Nathan). Then reopen the game and pick it here (Auto picks the best one).

Aiming is exact: darts hit where you tap (Easy adds a small helper ring; Normal has none). Head hits count double. With Blood splash off, darts only put dinosaurs to sleep (they snore) and nothing bleeds. The name, stars and settings stay on this iPad only. No accounts, ads or internet calls.

Lock the iPhone or iPad to this game: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On (set a passcode). In the game, triple-click the side or top button → Options → Time Limit.

↻
Turn the screen sideways to play. If it will not turn, switch off Rotation Lock.
